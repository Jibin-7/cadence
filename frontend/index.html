THE SEARCH MODEL

From candidate to ranking

Provider results are normalized into song, artist, album and genre fields. An inverted index maps each token to the live result documents that contain it. Boolean queries combine those posting lists with intersection, union and difference.

For vector ranking, field token counts are weighted and scored with TF-IDF and cosine-style normalization. Titles receive weight 3.0, artists 2.4, albums 1.5 and genres 1.0 because a match in the track name or performer is usually stronger evidence of intent. Exact-title and phrase signals are displayed separately.

FEEDBACK & EVALUATION

Your judgment changes order

Relevant and non-relevant marks are retained per search. The reranker combines the original query score with similarity to relevant examples and a penalty for non-relevant examples (Rocchio-style α/β/γ adjustment).

Precision@K, Recall@K, F1 and MRR use only recorded judgments. Search results without labels are not counted as known relevant or irrelevant.

What the provider supplies

Apple iTunes Search provides live track and album metadata, artwork, store URLs, and sometimes a 30-second preview. Lyrics and full-track playback are not provided. Preview audio is streamed directly from Apple when available and shown with Apple attribution and a nearby store link. Provider API requests are cached and paced at about one request every 3 seconds to stay below Apple’s documented approximate limit of 20 calls per minute.
